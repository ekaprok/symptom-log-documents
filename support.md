## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
To change or cancel your Symptom Log Pro subscription, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, and then tap Subscriptions. From there, you can view, change, or cancel your plan.

### How do I request a refund?
Because payments are processed by Apple, we cannot issue refunds directly. You can request a refund by visiting [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and logging in with your Apple ID.

### I found a bug. What should I do?
Please send an email to our support address above. Let us know what device you're using (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro), your iOS version, and the steps to reproduce the bug. Screenshots are always helpful!

### I have a feature request!
We'd love to hear it. Please email us with your ideas on how we can improve the app.
